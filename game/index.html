The First Turn

You'll need all four movement runes—UP, DOWN, LEFT, and RIGHT—to solve this one.

THE LOCKED LIBRARY

Magic is only a language.

Guide the ember from its outer chamber to the lock's heart. Each movement rune advances the ember the number of spaces held in the register. The register value starts at 1, but runes can change it. Negative values reverse the direction; zero holds the ember still. Each lock grants its own fireweight; movement runes consume 3 shingles of fireweight, while increment and decrement consume 1. Hitting stone or running out of fireweight ends the act of magic; the heart completes the trial.

ᛞDOWNMove south by register · 3 shingles
ᛈLEFTMove west by register · 3 shingles
ᚱRIGHTMove east by register · 3 shingles
ᛏINCAdd 1 to register · 1 shingle
ᚾDECSubtract 1 from register · 1 shingle
