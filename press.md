## Short Bio

Henry Coles has been a part of several new writer award lists, including the SCBWI Undiscovered Voices, Scottish Book Trust New Writer Awardees and The Times/Chicken House Prize.

He grew up in a small village near Bradford, surrounded by sheep and hills. He always wanted to write, but instead spent his first 40 years programming computers. He still programs computers, but now writes books as well – Traitor's Magic is his debut.
